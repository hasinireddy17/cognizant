Community Bulletin

The community continues to organize educational programs, health awareness campaigns, sports events, environmental initiatives and cultural celebrations throughout the year. Residents are encouraged to participate and contribute ideas for future development projects. Several new facilities are being planned including parks, reading centers and youth training workshops. Volunteers play a major role in ensuring the success of these initiatives and helping build stronger connections among residents.
